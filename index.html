About BNI Tathastu

BNI Tathastu is a Jaipur-based chapter of BNI, the world's leading networking organization. Our members meet weekly to exchange qualified business referrals and build lasting professional relationships.

We embody BNI's core values: Givers Gain®, Building Relationships, Lifelong Learning, Traditions + Innovation, Positive Attitude, Accountability, and Recognition.

Our Mission

To help members increase their business through a structured, positive, and professional word-of-mouth program that enables them to develop long-term, meaningful relationships with quality business professionals.
