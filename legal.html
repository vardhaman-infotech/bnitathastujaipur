Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information

Last Updated: January 15, 2025

BNI Tathastu is committed to protecting your privacy and personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit our website or interact with our chapter.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us through:

  • Visitor registration forms
  • Membership application forms
  • Contact forms and inquiries
  • Event registration forms
  • Newsletter subscriptions

Information Types

The types of personal information we may collect include:

  • Name and contact information (email, phone, address)
  • Business information (company name, profession, industry)
  • Professional background and interests
  • Communication preferences

How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information solely for BNI Tathastu's purposes, including:

  • Contacting interested visitors about chapter activities
  • Processing membership applications and inquiries
  • Communicating about meetings, events, and chapter updates
  • Providing requested information about BNI and our chapter
  • Managing member relationships and chapter operations

BNI Network Sharing

Your information may be shared within the BNI network, including:

  • BNI Regional offices for membership processing
  • BNI Global for system integration and member services
  • Other BNI chapters for referral and networking purposes (with consent)

Data Security & Storage

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information:

  • Secure data storage with encryption protocols
  • Limited access to authorized personnel only
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Secure transmission of sensitive information

Third-Party Integration

We may securely transmit your information to third-party services for operational purposes:

  • Google Sheets for record-keeping and data management
  • Email marketing platforms for chapter communications
  • BNI Connect systems for member management

Note: We do not share your personal information with third parties outside the BNI network for marketing or commercial purposes without your explicit consent.

Cookies & Website Analytics

Cookie Usage

Our website uses cookies for the following purposes:

  • Website analytics through Google Analytics 4 (GA4)
  • User login sessions (for member areas)
  • Improving user experience and site functionality
  • Remembering user preferences

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website. This service may collect:

  • Pages visited and time spent on site
  • Geographic location (city/country level)
  • Device and browser information
  • Referral sources

Cookie Control

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. By using our website, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy.

Your Rights & Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request information about what personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from communications at any time
  • Portability: Request a copy of your data in a portable format

Contact Us for Privacy Concerns

For any privacy-related questions, data deletion requests, or to exercise your rights, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@bnitathastu.com
  • Phone: +91 98765 43210
  • Address: BNI Tathastu, Jaipur, Rajasthan

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. When we make significant changes:

  • We will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • We will notify you via email or website notice
  • We will obtain consent for material changes that affect how we use your information

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.

Consent & Agreement

By using our website, submitting forms, or engaging with BNI Tathastu services, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to this Privacy Policy.

If you do not agree with any part of this Privacy Policy, please do not use our website or provide us with your personal information.

Questions or Concerns?

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please don't hesitate to contact us using the information provided above.
