Our Chapter Story

BNI Tathastu was founded in 2023 as part of BNI's Jaipur North region, with a vision to create a thriving community of business professionals committed to helping each other succeed through structured networking and quality referrals.

At the core of BNI Tathastu is the philosophy of Givers Gain® – by helping others, we all grow. We practice structured networking and adhere to BNI's Code of Ethics to ensure trust and quality business referrals among our members.

What makes us unique is our commitment to excellence and our record as one of the fastest-growing chapters in the region. Our members have collectively generated over ₹70 Crores in business through referrals, demonstrating the power of authentic relationship building.
